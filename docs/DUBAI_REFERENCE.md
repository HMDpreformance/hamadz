# Real Dubai reference for the game city

What is really in each area of our map, and what we build from it. Look-alikes only: no real brand names or logos in the game.
Started 2026-11-17. Parts marked (search) come from a web search that day; the rest is general knowledge of the city and should be checked against photos when an area gets built.

## Areas in part 2 (east half, later)

Business Bay (glass towers along a canal), the Creek (old trading boats, water taxis), Deira (older dense blocks, souks, neon shop signs), Ras Al Khor (wetland with flamingos, industrial yards), Nad Al Sheba (horse racing track with a long grandstand), the airport (terminals, planes landing low over the road), a big picture-frame shaped building near the creek.

## Streets

- Roads are wide and smooth. Highways have 5 to 7 lanes each way with a concrete barrier in the middle.
- Tall grey highway light poles in the median, double arm. Smaller poles on streets. Warm white light.
- Date palms in rows along boulevards and medians, with lights wrapped around the trunks at night. Flower beds and grass strips on main roads.
- Kerbs painted in black and white or black and yellow stripes.
- Blue direction signs (white text, Arabic above English) on streets. Green signs on highways. Brown signs for tourist places. Street name signs are getting new colours per area from late 2026 (search).
- Bus shelters are closed glass boxes with air conditioning (search).
- Roundabouts with fountains or art in older areas. Speed cameras on grey poles. Pedestrian bridges over wide roads.
- Petrol stations with big flat canopies in blue or in green and blue, each with a small shop.
- Sand everywhere land is empty. Building sites with cranes are common.

## Shops (look-alike names only)

Cafeteria and juice shops with bright signs, shawarma places, karak tea kiosks, small groceries, laundries, barber shops, phone shops, car accessory, tyre and window tint shops, car wash, pharmacies, perfume and abaya shops, big supermarkets, malls with valet parking. Signs are in Arabic and English together.

## Traffic cars (look-alikes, no logos)

- Big white 4x4s are the classic sight. A large Japanese 4x4 is the best selling vehicle in the country, and pickups are near the top (search).
- White is by far the most common colour. Then silver, black, grey.
- White and cream mid-size sedans, small Asian SUVs, minibuses, delivery motorbikes, water tankers, cement trucks.
- Taxis: cream body with a coloured roof. Red roof is the most common, also yellow, green, blue, orange (search).
- Police cars: white with dark green. The police also show off supercars.
- Night car scene: supercars, American muscle, tuned Japanese cars, lifted 4x4s and trucks.

## Night look

Warm street light on wet or dusty roads, towers with LED lines and animated facades, neon shop strips in the older areas, lit palms, a hazy orange sky near the horizon, sea and canal reflections.
